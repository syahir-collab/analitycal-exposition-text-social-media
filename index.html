What is an analytical exposition?

An analytical exposition is a text that persuades the reader that something is the case. The writer states a position and supports it with reasons.

Analytical exposition adalah teks yang bertujuan meyakinkan pembaca bahwa suatu hal memang benar atau penting. Penulis menyampaikan pendapat, lalu mendukungnya dengan alasan.

Social function: to persuade the reader that something is the case.

Analytical vs hortatory exposition

Analytical expositionHortatory exposition
PurposePersuade that something is the casePersuade that something should or should not be done
Example thesisDigital literacy is essential for social media users.Schools should teach digital literacy to every student.
StagesThesis, arguments, reiterationThesis, arguments, recommendation

Digital literacy on social media

Digital literacy is the ability to find, understand, evaluate, create, and share information safely and responsibly through digital technology.

Literasi digital adalah kemampuan menemukan, memahami, menilai, membuat, dan membagikan informasi dengan aman dan bertanggung jawab melalui teknologi digital. Rumusan ini disederhanakan dari kerangka literasi digital UNESCO (2018).

Why Digital Literacy Matters on Social MediaAnalytical exposition, contoh teks orisinal

Social media is part of daily life for many teenagers. We use it to chat with friends, find information, and share our ideas. In my opinion, digital literacy is essential for every social media user. There are three reasons why. It helps us avoid false information, protect our privacy, and communicate respectfully.

Firstly, digital literacy helps users recognise false information. Anyone can post anything on social media, so not every message is true. A digitally literate user checks the source and the date before believing a post. As a result, he or she does not spread hoaxes that may confuse other people.

Secondly, digital literacy protects personal privacy. Photos, locations, and personal details that we post can stay online for a long time. Moreover, strangers may misuse this information. Therefore, literate users set their accounts carefully and think before they share private data.

Thirdly, digital literacy encourages respectful communication. Harsh comments can hurt real people even though they are written on a screen. Users who understand digital ethics choose their words carefully and do not join in cyberbullying. In addition, they can use social media to support others.

In conclusion, social media offers many benefits, but those benefits are safest for users who are digitally literate. Because digital literacy helps us verify information, protect our privacy, and communicate politely, every user should develop it.

Ringkasan isi teks (Bahasa Indonesia)
Penulis berpendapat bahwa literasi digital sangat penting bagi pengguna media sosial. Ada tiga alasan: literasi digital membantu mengenali informasi palsu, melindungi privasi, dan mendorong komunikasi yang santun. Di akhir, penulis menegaskan kembali bahwa manfaat media sosial paling aman bagi pengguna yang melek digital, sehingga setiap pengguna sebaiknya mengembangkannya.

Key takeaways

  • An analytical exposition persuades the reader that something is the case.
  • Its structure has three stages: thesis, arguments, and reiteration.
  • It uses simple present tense, connectives, modal verbs, and evaluative words.
  • Digital literacy helps social media users check facts, protect privacy, and communicate politely.
